⚡ 1. The Astral Loom

Theme: Fate and cosmic order
Prime Deity: The Weaver Eternal, who spins the threads of all existence.
Other Gods:

Kheran the Spindle – god of structure and inevitability.

Tiriel the Severed Thread – goddess of rebellion and free will.

Vaelith the Patterned Eye – god of prophecy and foresight.

Senn the Golden Needle – god of repair, destiny’s healer.

Factions:

The Spindlers: Devotees of Kheran, believing fate is absolute and must never be altered.

The Cutters: Followers of Tiriel, who teach that mortals must learn to weave their own destinies, even if it tears the cosmic fabric.

🔥 2. The Ember Faith

Theme: Renewal through destruction
Prime Deity: Pyra, the Dawnflame, goddess of rebirth through fire.
Other Gods:

Azel the Forgeheart – god of endurance and craftsmanship.

Vathor the Cinder King – god of righteous wrath.

Saalis the Smoke Dancer – goddess of transformation and illusion.

Ruun the Ashfather – god of endings, keeper of cooled embers.

Factions:

The Burning Path: Zealots of Vathor who seek purification through conquest and fire.

The Ashen Circle: Followers of Ruun who preach patience — letting destruction settle before renewal begins.

🌒 3. The Veiled Mother

Theme: Secrets, dreams, and hidden truths
Prime Deity: The Veiled Mother, mistress of sleep and keeper of the unseen world.
Other Gods:

Nyssra the Whisperer – goddess of secrets and seduction.

Korthal the Dream-Eater – god of nightmares and forgotten truths.

Melyne the Silent Step – goddess of spies and lost souls.

Orren the Lantern-Bearer – god of revelation within dreams.

Factions:

The Silent Order: Serve Korthal and Nyssra, guarding secrets and manipulating dreams to shape destiny.

The Lantern Guild: Serve Orren, seeking to interpret dreams to illuminate truth rather than conceal it.

🌊 4. The Tides Eternal

Theme: Cycles of creation and destruction
Prime Deity: Thalmaris, Lord of the Deep, the ocean that remembers everything.
Other Gods:

Eira of the Waves – goddess of life, birth, and mercy.

Vorrak the Undertow – god of death, storms, and the pull to the abyss.

Seral the Driftwood King – god of fate and wandering souls.

Luneth the Mirror Tide – goddess of reflection and illusion.

Factions:

The High Tide: Devotees of Eira, embracing life, fertility, and renewal.

The Undertide: Worshippers of Vorrak, who view destruction as the ocean’s necessary breath.

⚙️ 5. The Crucible of Forms

Theme: Evolution through struggle
Prime Deity: Kraedon, the Godsmith, who reforged his own soul to perfect creation.
Other Gods:

Mora the Anvil-Mother – goddess of endurance and protection.

Ithar the Shard – god of pain and transformation.

Belka the Flamechild – goddess of inspiration and innovation.

Durn the Hammerhand – god of war and craft.

Factions:

The Forged Path: Disciples of Mora, emphasizing endurance, patience, and humility in one’s trials.

The Shardborn: Followers of Ithar, believing true divinity comes only through suffering and self-destruction.

🕯️ 6. The Silent Choir

Theme: Light, silence, and divine truth
Prime Deity: Solenne the Wordless, radiant being of pure light and stillness.
Other Gods:

Calor the Candlebearer – god of mercy and inner illumination.

Eshra the Radiant Hand – goddess of healing and revelation.

Veyn the Echo – god of forbidden speech and temptation.

Arthis the Glass Saint – god of discipline and contemplation.

Factions:

The Still Tongue: Devotees of Solenne and Arthis who practice absolute silence and meditation.

The Luminous Word: Priests of Veyn who claim speech itself can purify darkness, preaching through sacred chant.

🐍 7. The Serpent Beneath

Theme: Knowledge through decay and rebirth
Prime Deity: Oroth, the World-Serpent, whose coils encircle all life.
Other Gods:

Veliss the Molter – goddess of renewal and reincarnation.

Kaarn the Venom King – god of forbidden wisdom.

Nai’Thra the Burrower – goddess of earth and secrets below.

Zereth the Devourer – god of final endings, the Serpent’s fanged shadow.

Factions:

The Molten Coil: Devotees of Veliss, seeking to use decay to nurture new life and wisdom.

The Fanged Path: Followers of Kaarn and Zereth who pursue enlightenment through poison, death, and corruption.

🌞 8. The Radiant Accord

Theme: Divine harmony and perfection
Prime Deity: Aurelion, the Dawnfather, who first sang creation into being.
Other Gods:

Serapha the Silver Voice – goddess of beauty and song.

Caelus the Golden Scale – god of justice and balance.

Imera the Morning Bell – goddess of inspiration and faith.

Dareth the Discordant – god of freedom and creative chaos.

Factions:

The Harmonics: Followers of Serapha and Caelus, enforcing divine order and discipline.

The Dissonants: Followers of Dareth and Imera, who believe imperfection and change are vital parts of harmony.

🌑 9. The Cult of the Hollow Sun

Theme: Nihilism, entropy, and false light
Prime Deity: Nulthar, the Hollow Sun, a devouring void masquerading as light.
Other Gods:

Vesh the Eclipse – god of endings and oblivion.

Talan the Shadow Prophet – god of deceit and prophecy.

Karae the Ash Maiden – goddess of despair and false hope.

Dravon the Drowned Star – god of fallen glory.

Factions:

The Eclipse Faith: Seek to extinguish all false lights, hastening the inevitable darkness.

The Ash Prophets: Believe the void must be worshipped, not hastened — that through surrender, the soul transcends entropy.

🌿 10. The Verdant Covenant

Theme: Reciprocity, nature, and the balance of life and death
Prime Deity: The Greenmother, the first sprout born from divine blood.
Other Gods:

Orun the Thorn King – god of predation and dominance.

Velara the Blooming Hand – goddess of fertility and mercy.

Thess the Rotfather – god of decay and fungal rebirth.

Lyss the Wandering Seed – goddess of change and wild freedom.

Factions:

The Thornbound: Worshippers of Orun, who see nature as tooth and claw — survival of the fittest.

The Bloomwardens: Disciples of Velara, protecting the balance and healing the wounds left by greed or war.
