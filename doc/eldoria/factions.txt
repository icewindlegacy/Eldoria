====================================================================
                             GODS OF ELDORIA
====================================================================
The world of Eldoria is shaped by five great pantheons, each born
from the clash of Light and Shadow.  Their worshippers vie for
influence across the realms, their faiths often rival yet entwined.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SOLARI DOMINION  (Light, Order, Fire)
  Prime:  Aurelion – Radiant King, Lord of Light
  Seraphis – War, Justice
  Velara – Flame, Passion
  Thane – Sun, Valor
  Eryndor – Dawn, Prophecy

  Factions:
    Dawnwardens (Aurelion’s crusaders)
    Ember Choir (Velara’s mystics)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
TIDESWORN FAITH  (Water, Moon, Secrets)
  Prime:  Nerathis – Deep Mother, Keeper of the Moon
  Thalor – Storms, Travel
  Syrune – Dreams, Fate
  Coren – Tempest, Wrath
  Lethara – Calm, Mercy

  Factions:
    Abyssal Choir (Nerathis’ seers)
    Wavebound Order (Thalor’s sailors)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDANT ACCORD  (Nature, Renewal, Beasts)
  Prime:  Sylara – Lifeweaver, Mother of Growth
  Druven – Hunt, Beasts
  Faelen – Forests, Spirits
  Mirae – Fertility, Grace
  Korren – Decay, Rebirth

  Factions:
    Circle of Roots (Sylara’s druids)
    Thornbound (Korren’s wardens)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
FORGEBOUND CREED  (Earth, Craft, Fire)
  Prime:  Ornak – The Maker, Lord of the Forge
  Tavros – Guardianship, Fire
  Helra – Stone, Patience
  Brunn – Metal, Endurance
  Ildra – Invention, Craft

  Factions:
    Iron Covenant (Ornak’s builders)
    Emberforged (Ildra’s artificers)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
UMBRACULT  (Shadow, Death, Rebirth)
  Prime:  Umbra – The Eater of Dawn, Keeper of Silence
  Nethra – Secrets, Illusion
  Veyne – Night, Assassins
  Korran – Entropy, Decay
  Lirath – Ghosts, Memory

  Factions:
    Nightveil (Umbra’s monks)
    Ashen Path (Lirath’s necromancers)
