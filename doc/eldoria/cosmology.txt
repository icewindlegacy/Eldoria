====================================================================
                    Divine Relationships & Balance
====================================================================

  Aurelion <> Umbra     – Eternal counterforces: Light and Shadow.
  Nerathis () Sylara    – Life and Water entwined; birth and flow.
  Ornak () Aurelion     – Fire of creation given form in stone.
  Umbra () Sylara       – Death feeds Life; decay renews.
  Nerathis <> Ornak     – Sea erodes Mountain, Mountain shapes River.
  All four pantheons owe origin to Light and Shadow fragments.
