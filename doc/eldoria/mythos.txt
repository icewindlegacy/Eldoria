🌍 The Mythos of Eldoria: The Fractured Loom
“All gods were once one thought — and that thought was light.”

⚙️ I. The First Thought — Birth of the Loom

Before time, there was The First Light, an infinite mind dreaming of order. Within its dreaming, threads of existence intertwined — this was the Astral Loom, from which reality itself was spun.

From the Loom’s first spark came The Weaver Eternal, who became the first consciousness of creation. To assist in weaving reality, the Weaver spun four emanations of Its own being:

Kheran Spindle, who set the laws of motion and time.

Vaelith Eye, who perceived every possible pattern.

Senn Needle, who bound the worlds together.

Tiriel Thread, who defied the design and cut her own strand — bringing chaos, and with it, free will.

Tiriel’s rebellion tore the Loom. Through that tear, raw flame and unshaped matter spilled forth — the birth of Eldoria, the world of imperfect creation.

====================================================================

🔥 II. The Flame and the Sea

From the molten chaos rose Pyra the Dawnflame, first daughter of the Loom’s unraveling. She breathed upon the world, setting its sun alight. Where her fire met the void, it birthed Thalmaris, Lord of the Deep, who quenched her fury with waves of memory.

The two became eternal opposites — Fire and Sea, Change and Reflection. Together they forged the first rhythm of Eldoria: the endless tide of creation and destruction.

Their children shaped the elements:

Eira Waves birthed life.

Vathor CinderKing tempered fire into metal and war.

Luneth MirrorTide captured light into illusions — the first dreams.

Thus the Ember Faith and Tides Eternal formed the first balance — one burns to renew, one drowns to preserve.
====================================================================

🌒 III. The Dreaming of the Veil

From Luneth’s illusions came thought and sleep. In this unseen realm stirred The Veiled Mother, the shadow of the Weaver’s mind that dreamed independently.

Her children ruled the unseen:

Nyssra Whisperer sowed secrets in mortal dreams.

Korthal DreamEater devoured truths best forgotten.

Orren LanternBearer illuminated the path between sleep and waking.

Her faithful claim that the Veiled Mother is the half of the Weaver that still sleeps — the part that dreams our reality into being.
====================================================================

⚙️ IV. The Crucible and the Smith

When Pyra’s flames cooled and Thalmaris’s seas hardened, Kraedon the Godsmith arose from the cooled crust of Eldoria. He discovered the world was flawed — brittle and impure — and thus swore to reforge it.

He struck the world upon his anvil, awakening the sparks of new divinity:

Mora AnvilMother shaped endurance.

Belka Flamechild gave passion and art.

Ithar Shard embodied pain as the tool of transformation.

Kraedon’s hammerblows echoed into the heavens, giving structure to the divine realms — but each blow further fractured the Loom’s original unity.
====================================================================

🕯️ V. The Silence and the Choir

From the ringing of Kraedon’s hammer came sound, and with it sorrow, for the gods began to hear their own imperfections.

In pity, the Weaver’s remaining light condensed into Solenne the Wordless, embodiment of divine stillness. Her followers, the Silent Choir, believe that only through silence can the Loom be mended.

Her companions:

Calor Candlebearer taught compassion.

Eshra RadiantHand healed the first wounded souls.

Veyn Echo, born of sound itself, whispered forbidden hymns that stirred rebellion among angels.

Some Choirs seek to silence all creation; others to teach harmony in quiet devotion.
====================================================================

🐍 VI. The Serpent Beneath

When Solenne’s light fell upon the earth, it revealed what lay below — Oroth the World-Serpent, coiled beneath all things. Some say he is the Weaver’s shadow; others that he is Tiriel’s fallen thread, grown sentient.

He dreamed of cycles: birth, decay, and rebirth.
From his molts came:

Veliss Molter, renewal.

Kaarn VenomKing, forbidden wisdom.

Naithra Burrower, secrets beneath the soil.

Oroth taught that knowledge grows from rot — and thus the faith of the Serpent Beneath sees beauty in entropy.
====================================================================

🌞 VII. The Radiant Accord

Long after fire, sea, and serpent shaped the world, a new light rose — Aurelion the Dawnfather, born from the Weaver’s lost spark. He sang creation into melody and forged the Radiant Accord, a pantheon devoted to harmony.

His choir included:

Serapha SilverVoice, song and beauty.

Caelus GoldenScale, justice and balance.

Imera MorningBell, faith and courage.

Dareth Discordant, creative chaos and individuality.

The Accord teaches that the universe is divine music — but discord is necessary to give the song meaning.
====================================================================

🌑 VIII. The Hollow Sun

As Aurelion’s light spread, his brilliance revealed the void left by the Weaver’s absence. From that void rose Nulthar the Hollow Sun, who claimed the light was false — that creation itself is merely the shadow of a greater nothingness.

He was followed by:

Vesh Eclipse, endings and oblivion.

Talan ShadowProphet, deceit and revelation.

Karae AshMaiden, despair and false hope.

Dravon DrownedStar, fallen glory.

The Cult of the Hollow Sun whispers that all gods are doomed reflections — and when the final light dies, truth will be seen.
====================================================================

🌿 IX. The Verdant Covenant

As gods quarreled and the world trembled, blood fell upon the soil. From it grew The Greenmother, born of divine sacrifice. Her roots reached the underworld; her leaves touched the stars.

She birthed balance through:

Orun ThornKing, predator and dominance.

Velara BloomingHand, mercy and fertility.

Thess Rotfather, decay and fungal rebirth.

Lyss WanderingSeed, change and chaos.

Her faith teaches the Covenant of Life — that every death feeds new life, every harvest must be repaid.
====================================================================

⚖️ X. The Great Fracture and the Present Age

At last, the gods realized they were not ten pantheons, but ten fragments of the Weaver Eternal — reflections of one broken divinity. The Loom itself, strained by their conflicts, frayed once more.

Some gods, like Aurelion and Solenne, seek to restore unity.

Others, like Tiriel and Nulthar, believe the fracture is freedom.

Oroth waits beneath, certain that all things will one day return to the soil — even the gods themselves.

Thus the world of Eldoria endures between opposing forces:
Order and Chaos, Light and Shadow, Growth and Decay — all threads of one divine tapestry, still being woven, still unraveling.
